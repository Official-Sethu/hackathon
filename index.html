WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

Why should you trust a fact-checker?

Most fact-checkers rely on just one AI — which means one opinion, one blind spot, one place to go wrong. VeritasGonka uses three completely separate AI systems that don't know what each other said — and only agrees when they all reach the same conclusion.

01

Three AIs, not one

Like getting three different doctors' opinions — if they all agree, you can be much more confident.

02

Every check is numbered

Each verification gets a unique ID you can keep. It proves the check actually happened and wasn't made up.

03

We show our working

You can see every step of the reasoning — not just the final answer. No hidden decisions.

04

Works for everyone

Available in multiple languages, with an audio read-out for accessibility and shareable cards for social media.
